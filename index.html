About

Reliable models over impressive numbers

I'm a Computer Science student at COMSATS University Islamabad, Sahiwal Campus, building toward AI/ML engineering and research. My work asks a simple question: does this model deserve to be trusted?

That means testing on external datasets, reporting multi-seed results, auditing preprocessing choices, and measuring calibration and uncertainty instead of stopping at headline accuracy. My projects span medical imaging (brain MRI, dermoscopy, histopathology), full-stack AI tooling and agent evaluation.

Ongoing research · manuscript in preparation

Toward Trustworthy AI for Brain Tumor MRI Classification

A multi-pillar clinical-readiness framework built on transfer-learning baselines for 4-class brain MRI (glioma, meningioma, pituitary, no tumor), evaluated beyond in-distribution accuracy.

  • Models: ResNet50, EfficientNetB0, MobileNetV2 with multi-seed evaluation (seeds 42, 123, 456); MobileNetV2 scored 94.00–94.50% on the Kaggle test set.
  • Cross-dataset: trained on 7,023 images, tested zero-shot on an external Figshare set; MobileNetV2 reached 86.13%.
  • Finding: EfficientNetB0's early ~25% accuracy came from generic rescaling instead of its architecture-specific preprocessing, a preprocessing-sensitivity pitfall.
  • Pillars: anatomical plausibility loss, conformal triage, Clinical Readiness Index, co-developed with a collaborator. Grad-CAM explainability throughout.

TrustworthyMed: Skin Lesion Reliability

Evaluates reliability, calibration and explainability of deep models for dermoscopic lesion classification under distribution shift.

  • Models hit 95.6% accuracy on benign nevi but only 41.2% on melanoma while staying highly confident on hard cases.
  • Class-conditional trust framework combining uncertainty, calibration, robustness, test-time adaptation and OOD detection.

SemiGAN-MelanoPath

Semi-supervised GAN for histopathology cancer detection under extreme label scarcity on BreakHis (7,909 images, 82 patients) with strict patient-level splits.

  • Combines VAT, rotation self-supervision, feature matching and MC-Dropout uncertainty.
  • 5-stage ablation across 1%–100% label budgets designed; full experiment matrix still to run.

MedAI Black Box

Full-stack forensic auditing tool for a ResNet18 brain tumor classifier. Each prediction goes through Grad-CAM / Integrated Gradients, perturbation robustness tests and uncertainty estimation.

  • A deterministic reliability engine returns TRUST / REVIEW / ABSTAIN instead of raw confidence.
  • Not a clinical tool.
